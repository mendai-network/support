Start with these common questions, or contact us for more help.

I’m having trouble signing in.

Check that you’re using the email address associated with your Mendai account. If you’ve forgotten your password, use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. Check your spam folder if the reset email hasn’t arrived.

My premium subscription isn’t showing.

Confirm that you’re signed in to the Mendai account used for your subscription and the Apple Account used for your purchase. Reopen the app and use Restore Purchases if available. If premium access is still missing, contact support with the purchase date and a screenshot of the issue. Never send your full payment details.

How do I cancel my iPhone subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Mendai and select Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app or your Mendai account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I find a Soul connection?

Ask your AI Guide to help you find someone with shared interests, or open the Souls tab and use Find Souls. Check your search preferences if you’re not finding suitable connections.

Messages, voice or calls aren’t working.

Check your internet connection and install the latest Mendai update. For voice or video features, check Mendai’s microphone and camera permissions in your device settings. If the issue continues, tell support your device model, app version and what happened.

How do I delete my account or ask about my data?

Open Mendai’s Settings and choose the account deletion option. You can also contact support with privacy or account questions. If you have an Apple subscription, manage or cancel it separately in your iPhone’s subscription settings.

Before you send

Include your app version, device model and a short description of the issue. Please don’t include passwords, verification codes or full card details. Hide private messages and personal information in screenshots.
